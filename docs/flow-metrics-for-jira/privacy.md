# Privacy Policy — Flow Metrics for Jira

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Effective Date:** 22 June 2026
**Marketplace:** Atlassian Marketplace

---

## 1. Overview

Flow Metrics for Jira ("the App") is published by Steelyman Lab ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over your data. We have written this policy in plain English on purpose — legal jargon helps no one.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

The App collects only the minimum data needed to provide its features. Specifically:

| Data | Description |
|---|---|
| **Jira issue metadata** | Issue key (e.g. `PROJ-123`), issue summary (title), and created date |
| **Status changelog timestamps** | The date and time each issue moved from one status to another |
| **User-supplied JQL strings** | Filters you type or save inside the App to scope your boards or reports |
| **Source labels** | Names or tags you assign to group or identify data sources within the App |

We do **not** collect:

- Jira issue descriptions, comments, or attachments
- Personal information about issue reporters or assignees (such as names or email addresses)
- Payment or billing information
- Any data unrelated to the features above

---

## 3. Why We Collect It

We use the data listed above for these specific purposes:

- **Delivering the App's core features** — calculating flow metrics (cycle time, throughput, work-in-progress, etc.) and displaying charts and reports based on your Jira data.
- **Saving your configuration** — storing your JQL filters and source labels so your settings persist between sessions.
- **Improving the App** — understanding usage patterns in aggregate (never individually) to prioritise improvements and fix bugs.

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it for any purpose beyond what is described here.

---

## 4. How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Jira issue metadata & changelog timestamps | Retained only while the App is installed. Deleted within **30 days** of uninstallation. |
| JQL strings & source labels | Retained only while the App is installed. Deleted within **30 days** of uninstallation. |
| Aggregate, anonymised usage statistics | Up to **12 months**, after which they are permanently deleted. |

You may also request deletion of your data at any time — see **Section 7** below.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run the App. Where any data is shared with these services, it is limited to what they strictly need to perform their function.

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Atlassian** | App hosting and Jira API access | Data flows through Atlassian's platform per their [Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy) |
| **Cloud hosting provider** (e.g. AWS or similar) | Secure infrastructure to run the App's backend | Encrypted Jira metadata and timestamps |

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any other third parties beyond those necessary to operate the App.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
- Encryption at rest
- Access controls limiting who within Steelyman Lab can view stored data

No system is 100% secure. If we ever discover a breach that affects your data, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights over your data:

### GDPR (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)
- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Correction** — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Restriction** — ask us to limit how we process your data.
- **Objection** — object to our processing where we rely on legitimate interests.

### CCPA (California residents)
- **Know** — request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected.
- **Delete** — request deletion of your personal information.
- **Opt-out of sale** — we do not sell personal information, so this right is already fulfilled by default.
- **Non-discrimination** — we will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address in **Section 8**. We will respond within **30 days** (or sooner where required by law).
