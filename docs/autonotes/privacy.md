# Privacy Policy — AutoNotes

**Effective Date:** May 23, 2026
**Publisher:** AutoNotes
**Marketplace:** Atlassian Marketplace

---

## 1. Overview

AutoNotes is a Jira app published on the Atlassian Marketplace. We take your privacy seriously and have built AutoNotes to collect only the minimum data needed to provide the service. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what your rights are.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

We collect two categories of data:

| Data Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Jira Issue Summaries** | The title/summary text of Jira issues you interact with inside AutoNotes. |
| **Anonymous Usage Counts** | Aggregate counts of feature interactions (e.g., how many times a feature is used). These counts contain **no personally identifiable information**. |

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email address, or account profile
- Jira issue descriptions, comments, or attachments
- Any payment or billing information
- IP addresses linked to individual users

---

## 3. Why We Collect It

| Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Jira Issue Summaries** | Processed on our servers in real time to generate notes and suggestions. This is the core function of the app. |
| **Anonymous Usage Counts** | Help us understand which features are useful so we can improve the product. |

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

---

## 4. How We Process Jira Issue Summaries

Jira issue summaries are sent to our servers **only for the duration of processing your request**. Once processing is complete and the result is returned to you:

- The summary text is **immediately discarded**
- It is **not written to a database**
- It is **not logged in any identifiable form**
- It is **not shared with any third party for storage**

In short: we process it, use it once, and throw it away.

---

## 5. How Long Data Is Kept

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| **Jira Issue Summaries** | Not stored. Discarded immediately after processing. |
| **Anonymous Usage Counts** | Retained indefinitely in aggregated, non-identifiable form. These cannot be tied back to you. |

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

AutoNotes may rely on the following types of third-party infrastructure:

- **Cloud Hosting / Infrastructure** — Our processing servers run on a cloud provider (such as AWS or similar). Data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Issue summaries pass through this infrastructure but are not persisted.
- **Atlassian APIs** — We interact with Jira through Atlassian's standard APIs as required to deliver the app's functionality. Atlassian's own privacy policy governs their data handling.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, analytics brokers, or any other third parties.

---

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest, including:

- TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your Jira instance and our servers
- Access controls limiting who can access infrastructure
- Regular review of our data handling practices

No system is 100% secure, but we are committed to handling your data responsibly.

---

## 8. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### GDPR (EU/EEA/UK Residents)
- **Right to access** — Ask what data we hold about you.
- **Right to erasure** — Request deletion of any personal data we hold.
- **Right to portability** — Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
- **Right to object** — Object to how we process your data.
- **Right to restrict processing** — Ask us to limit how we use your data.

### CCPA (California Residents)
- **Right to know** — Ask what personal information we've collected, used, or disclosed.
- **Right to delete** — Request deletion of personal information we hold.
- **Right to opt out** — We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.
- **Right to non-discrimination** — We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

> Because Jira issue summaries are **not stored**, there is typically no personal data for us to retrieve or delete after a session ends. Anonymous usage counts cannot be linked back to you and therefore cannot be deleted on an individual basis.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

AutoNotes is designed for business and professional use. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 16.
