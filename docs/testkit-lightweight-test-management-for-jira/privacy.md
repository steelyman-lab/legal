# Privacy Policy

**App name:** TestKit – Lightweight Test Management for Jira
**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Marketplace:** Atlassian Marketplace
**Effective date:** 11 June 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Steelyman Lab ("we", "us", or "our") built TestKit to help teams manage test cases
and track test execution directly inside Jira. This policy explains what data
TestKit collects, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you
have over it.

We keep things straightforward: we only collect what is needed to make the app
work, and we never sell your data.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you use TestKit, we store the following information:

| Data | Description |
|---|---|
| **Jira project key** | The identifier of the Jira project in which TestKit is used (e.g., `PROJ`). |
| **Jira issue keys** | Keys of Jira issues that are linked to test cases (e.g., `PROJ-42`). |
| **Atlassian account ID** | The unique Atlassian account ID of the user who runs a test execution. No name or email address is stored directly. |
| **Test case content** | Titles, step descriptions, and expected results that you author inside TestKit. |
| **Test execution results** | Pass/fail/blocked status and any free-text "actual results" you enter when running a test. |

We do **not** collect passwords, payment details, or any Jira data beyond what is
listed above.

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Data

All data listed above is collected solely to **provide and operate the TestKit
service**:

- **Project and issue keys** let us associate test cases with the correct Jira
  project and issues.
- **Atlassian account ID** lets us record who executed a test so teams can
  audit test history.
- **Test case content and execution results** are the core content of the app —
  without them, TestKit cannot function.

We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose
unrelated to running the app.

---

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, our
legal basis for processing your data is **legitimate interests** (Article 6(1)(f)
GDPR) — specifically, the legitimate interest of providing the software service
you or your organisation has installed. Where required, we rely on your
organisation's acceptance of the Atlassian Marketplace terms as the contractual
basis for processing.

---

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Test cases and execution results | Kept for as long as your Atlassian instance has TestKit installed and active. |
| All data | Deleted within **30 days** of you uninstalling TestKit from your Atlassian instance, or sooner upon a verified deletion request (see Section 8). |

We do not retain data in backups beyond **60 days** after deletion.

---

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on infrastructure provided by **Atlassian** (via Atlassian
Forge, the platform on which TestKit is built). This means:

- Data is processed and stored within Atlassian's Forge environment, which is
  subject to Atlassian's own security and compliance standards.
- We do not transfer your data to any separate servers or databases operated by
  Steelyman Lab.
- Atlassian's data centres are certified to industry standards (ISO 27001,
  SOC 2). See [Atlassian's Trust & Security page](https://www.atlassian.com/trust)
  for details.

We apply the principle of least privilege — TestKit only requests the Jira
scopes it needs to operate.

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

TestKit runs entirely on the **Atlassian Forge** platform. We do not share your
data with any other third-party services, analytics providers, or advertising
networks.

Because Atlassian Forge hosts and executes the app, Atlassian processes data on
our behalf as a sub-processor. This is governed by
[Atlassian's Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).

---

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding
your personal data:

**Everyone**
- **Access** – Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
- **Deletion** – Ask us to delete your data.

**EEA / UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)**
- **Rectification** – Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Restriction** – Ask us to limit how we process your data.
- **Portability** – Request your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Object** – Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Lodge a complaint** – You have the right to complain to your local data
  protection authority.

**California residents (CCPA / CPRA)**
- **Know** – Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of
  personal information we have collected.
- **Delete** – Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Correct** – Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
- **Non-discrimination** – We will not discriminate against you for exercising
  any of these rights.
- We do **not** sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural
  advertising.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **[CONTACT_EMAIL]**. We
will respond within **30 days** (or sooner where required by law).
