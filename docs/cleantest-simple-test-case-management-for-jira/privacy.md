# Privacy Policy

**App:** CleanTest — Simple Test Case Management for Jira
**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Marketplace:** Atlassian Marketplace
**Effective Date:** June 10, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Steelyman Lab built CleanTest to be a simple, focused tool for managing test cases inside Jira. We take privacy seriously, and we've designed CleanTest with a straightforward principle: **we do not collect your data.**

This policy explains what that means in practice, what third-party services are involved, and what rights you have.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

**We collect no personal data.**

CleanTest does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information about you or your users. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Names, email addresses, or account identifiers
- Usage analytics or behavioral tracking
- Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry
- Cookies or tracking pixels
- IP addresses or device identifiers

All data created within CleanTest (such as test cases and test runs) is stored exclusively within your own Jira instance, governed by your existing Atlassian agreement. Steelyman Lab never has access to that data.

---

## 3. Why We Collect No Data

CleanTest is a lightweight Jira app. Everything it needs to function lives inside your Jira environment. There is no backend service operated by Steelyman Lab, and no reason for us to receive or retain any of your information.

---

## 4. Data Retention

Because we collect no data, we have no data to retain. There is nothing stored on Steelyman Lab servers or infrastructure.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

CleanTest does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

The app runs inside the **Atlassian platform** (Jira Cloud, Data Center, or Server, depending on your installation). Your use of Jira and the Atlassian Marketplace is governed by Atlassian's own Privacy Policy, available at [https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Steelyman Lab has no control over, and no responsibility for, Atlassian's data practices.

---

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Even though we do not collect personal data, we want to be clear about your rights under applicable privacy laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to:

- Provide access to
- Correct or update
- Delete
- Port to another service
- Sell (we do not sell any personal data)

If you believe we may hold data about you and wish to make a request, you are welcome to contact us and we will respond promptly.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

CleanTest is a professional productivity tool intended for use in business environments. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on the Atlassian Marketplace listing and update the effective date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Any changes will take effect as soon as the updated policy is published.
