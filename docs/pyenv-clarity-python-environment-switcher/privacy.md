# Privacy Policy — PyEnv Clarity (Python Environment Switcher)

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Marketplace:** Visual Studio Code Marketplace
**Effective Date:** 2026-06-06

---

## 1. Overview

PyEnv Clarity is a Visual Studio Code extension that helps you switch between
Python environments quickly and easily. This Privacy Policy explains what
information the extension handles (spoiler: essentially none), why, and what
your rights are.

We believe privacy should be the default — not an afterthought.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

**We collect no personal data whatsoever.**

PyEnv Clarity operates entirely on your local machine. It reads information
that is already available inside your VS Code workspace — such as the list of
Python interpreters detected on your system — and uses it only to display
options within the editor. None of this information ever leaves your device.

Specifically, we do **not** collect, transmit, or store:

- Your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers
- Your IP address or device identifiers
- Usage statistics, telemetry, or crash reports
- File paths, project names, or workspace contents
- Any data about the Python environments on your machine

---

## 3. Why We Don't Collect Data

PyEnv Clarity does its job entirely locally. There is no backend server,
no analytics pipeline, and no account system. Because we have no need for your
data to make the extension work, we simply don't collect it.

---

## 4. How Long Data Is Kept

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to retain. No records of your
usage are created by this extension at any point.

VS Code itself may store extension settings (e.g., your preferred interpreter)
in your local workspace or user settings files. That data lives on your own
machine, under your own control, and is governed by Microsoft's VS Code privacy
policy — not ours.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

PyEnv Clarity does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising,
monitoring, or data-processing services.

The only third-party touchpoint is the **Visual Studio Code Marketplace**,
which handles distribution of the extension. Any data collected by Microsoft
in connection with the Marketplace (e.g., download counts) is subject to
[Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).
Steelyman Lab does not receive any personal data from Microsoft as part of
this process.

---

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Even though we hold no personal data, we want to be transparent about your
rights under applicable privacy laws.

### Under GDPR (EU/EEA/UK residents)
You have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
- **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — request deletion of your personal
  data.
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Portability** — receive your data in a portable format.
- **Objection** — object to how we process your data.

Because we hold no personal data, fulfilling any of these requests is
effectively instantaneous — there is nothing for us to access, delete, or
correct. You are still welcome to contact us and we will confirm this in
writing.

### Under CCPA (California residents)
You have the right to:
- Know what personal information we collect, use, or share.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Opt out of the sale of your personal information.

We do not collect, sell, or share personal information. California residents
can confirm this by contacting us at the address below.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

PyEnv Clarity is a developer tool not directed at children. We do not
knowingly collect any information from anyone under the age of 13 (or the
applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). Given that we collect no data
from anyone, this is satisfied by design.
