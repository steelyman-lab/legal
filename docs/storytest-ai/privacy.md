# Privacy Policy — StoryTest AI

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Marketplace:** Atlassian Marketplace
**Effective Date:** 2026-05-24

---

## 1. Overview

StoryTest AI ("the App") is a Jira plugin published by **Steelyman Lab** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use the App, why we collect it, how we handle it, and what rights you have over it.

We keep this policy short and plain on purpose. If something is unclear, please reach out — details in [Section 8](#8-contact--deletion-requests).

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you use StoryTest AI inside Jira, we may process the following data:

| Data Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Jira Issue Summary** | The title/summary field of a Jira issue you open in the App |
| **Jira Issue Description** | The body/description field of that issue |
| **Acceptance Criteria Field Contents** | The content of any acceptance criteria field attached to the issue |
| **Workspace Usage Counts** | Aggregate counts of how often the App is used within a Jira workspace (e.g., number of generations per workspace) |

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email address, or personal account details
- Any Jira data outside of the fields listed above
- Payment information
- Data from issues you do not actively open within the App

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Data

| Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Issue Summary, Description, Acceptance Criteria | Sent to an AI model to generate test cases and suggestions relevant to the issue |
| Workspace Usage Counts | Used to monitor App health, enforce fair-use limits, and improve the product |

We process this data solely to **provide and improve the App's core functionality**. We do not use your Jira content for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to test-case generation.

---

## 4. How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Issue content (Summary, Description, Acceptance Criteria) | **Not stored.** This data is passed to the AI model in real time and is not persisted on our servers after the response is returned. |
| Workspace Usage Counts | Retained for up to **12 months**, then deleted or anonymised. |

We do not build profiles from your issue content or store it in any database.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

To generate test cases, the App sends issue content to a third-party AI provider:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenAI** (or equivalent LLM provider) | AI-powered test case generation | Issue summary, description, and acceptance criteria |

When data is sent to a third-party AI provider:
- It is sent over an encrypted (TLS) connection.
- The provider may retain it briefly for safety and moderation purposes, subject to their own privacy policy.
- We do **not** authorise providers to use your Jira content to train their models (we rely on data-processing agreements or API terms that exclude training use).

We recommend reviewing the relevant provider's privacy policy. If you have questions about which provider is active, contact us at the address in [Section 8](#8-contact--deletion-requests).

---

## 6. Data Security

- All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- We follow Atlassian's security guidelines for Marketplace apps.
- Access to usage data is restricted to authorised Steelyman Lab personnel only.
- We do not sell your data to any third party, ever.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights:

### GDPR (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland)
- **Access** — Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")** — Ask us to delete your data.
- **Restriction** — Ask us to stop processing your data in certain circumstances.
- **Portability** — Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection** — Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK or your EU member state's authority).

### CCPA (California, USA)
- **Know** — Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect.
- **Delete** — Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Opt-Out of Sale** — We do not sell personal information, so no opt-out is required.
- **Non-Discrimination** — We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, see [Section 8](#8-contact--deletion-requests) below.
