# Privacy Policy

# PunchCard — One-Click Jira Time Logger

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Marketplace:** Chrome Web Store
**Effective Date:** June 15, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PunchCard — One-Click Jira Time Logger ("PunchCard", "the Extension", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Chrome extension. We believe privacy should be simple. The short version: **we collect nothing.**

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

**None.**

PunchCard does not collect, transmit, store, share, or sell any personal data — ever. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Names, email addresses, or account identifiers
- Browsing history or activity
- Jira credentials, tokens, or project data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Device information or IP addresses
- Cookies or tracking identifiers

All interactions between PunchCard and your Jira instance happen **directly in your browser**, between your machine and your Jira server. No data passes through our servers because we have no servers involved in the Extension's operation.

---

## 3. Why We Collect No Data

PunchCard is a simple utility. It does one thing — log time to Jira in one click. It does not need a backend, does not require an account with us, and has no business reason to handle your personal information.

---

## 4. How Long Data Is Kept

Because we collect no data, there is nothing to retain. No data is stored on our end at any point.

Any settings or preferences you configure within the Extension (such as saved Jira URLs or default time entries) are stored **locally on your device** using Chrome's built-in storage API (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your machine and can be cleared at any time by removing the Extension.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

PunchCard does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

The only external service PunchCard communicates with is **your own Jira instance** — the URL you configure yourself. We have no affiliation with Atlassian and this communication is entirely direct, between your browser and your Jira server.

---

## 6. Permissions

PunchCard may request certain Chrome browser permissions (such as access to specific URLs) solely to enable its core functionality of logging time to Jira. No permission is used for data collection or tracking purposes.

---
