## 1. Overview

ClearTest is a Jira app built by Steelyman Lab that helps teams create, manage, and execute test cases directly inside Jira. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use ClearTest, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it.

We keep this policy in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, please reach out to us at [CONTACT_EMAIL].

---

## 2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to anyone who interacts with ClearTest inside a Jira instance — including test authors, testers, and Jira administrators at organizations that have installed the app.

---

## 3. What Data We Collect

ClearTest collects only the data it needs to function. Here is exactly what that is:

| Data | Description |
|---|---|
| **Jira Account IDs** | The internal Atlassian account identifier of users who create or execute test cases. This is not your name or email — it is an opaque ID assigned by Atlassian. |
| **Test Case Content** | Free-text content you write inside test cases (e.g., test steps, expected results, descriptions). |
| **Test Execution Records** | The status of a test run (e.g., Pass, Fail, Blocked) and any comments you attach to an execution. |
| **Project & Issue Keys** | Jira project keys (e.g., `MYPROJ`) and issue keys (e.g., `MYPROJ-42`) used to link test cases to Jira issues. |

**We do not collect** names, email addresses, passwords, payment information, or any data outside the Jira context listed above.

---

## 4. Why We Collect This Data

Every piece of data we collect has a specific purpose:

- **Jira Account IDs** — To record who authored or ran a test, enabling audit trails and attribution within your team.
- **Test Case Content** — To store and display your test cases so they persist across sessions and are accessible to your team.
- **Test Execution Records** — To track test run history, report on quality trends, and let teams see pass/fail status over time.
- **Project & Issue Keys** — To link test cases to the relevant Jira issues and display contextual test results alongside those issues.

**Legal basis (GDPR):** We process this data based on **legitimate interests** (enabling the core functionality of a tool your organization has chosen to install) and, where applicable, **performance of a contract** (the Atlassian Marketplace terms agreed to by your organization's administrator).

---

## 5. How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Test case content, execution records, project/issue links | Kept for as long as the app is installed on your Jira instance. |
| Jira Account IDs in historical records | Retained with the records they are attached to for the same period. |
| All data | Permanently deleted within **30 days** after the app is uninstalled from your Jira instance. |

You can also request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

---

## 6. How We Store and Protect Your Data

- All data is stored using **Atlassian Forge** infrastructure (where applicable) or secured cloud storage with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
- Access to stored data is restricted to Steelyman Lab personnel who need it for support or maintenance purposes.
- We do not sell your data. Ever.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

### For Everyone
- **Access** — Ask us what data we hold that is associated with your Jira Account ID.
- **Deletion** — Request that we delete your data. We will process deletion requests within **30 days**.
- **Correction** — Ask us to correct inaccurate data we hold about you.

### GDPR (EEA, UK, Switzerland)
In addition to the above, you have the right to:
- **Restrict or object** to processing of your data.
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Lodge a complaint** with your local data protection authority (e.g., your national DPA).

### CCPA (California Residents)
You have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected and how it is used.
- Request deletion of your personal information.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

We do not sell personal information, so there is no opt-out needed for data sales.
