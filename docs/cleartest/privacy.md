# Privacy Policy for ClearTest

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026
**Marketplace:** Atlassian Marketplace (Jira)

---

## 1. Overview

ClearTest is a test management app for Jira, built and maintained by Steelyman Lab. We take your privacy seriously and aim to be straightforward about what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it. This policy explains our practices in plain English.

This policy applies to all users of ClearTest installed via the Atlassian Marketplace.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you use ClearTest, we collect and store the following data:

| Data | Description |
|---|---|
| **Jira Issue Keys & Summaries** | The identifiers (e.g., `PROJ-123`) and titles of Jira issues linked to test cases |
| **Test Step Text** | The action steps and expected results you write inside test cases |
| **Test Execution Status** | Pass, fail, blocked, or other status values recorded during test runs |
| **Comments** | Any comments added to test steps or test executions |
| **Evidence URLs** | URLs pasted as evidence during test execution (e.g., links to screenshots or logs) |
| **Jira Account IDs** | The Atlassian account ID of users who execute tests, used to record who ran a test |

We do **not** collect passwords, payment information, or any data outside the scope listed above.

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Data

We collect this data solely to provide the core functionality of ClearTest:

- **Issue keys and summaries** are used to link test cases to your Jira issues and display meaningful context within the app.
- **Test step text** is the core content of your test cases — we store it so your team can view, edit, and reuse it.
- **Execution status and comments** let your team track test run outcomes and collaborate on results.
- **Evidence URLs** are stored to give testers a way to attach proof of testing to a specific execution.
- **Jira account IDs** are used to show who executed a test and to support audit trails within your project.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the app.

---

## 4. How Long We Keep Your Data

- Data is retained **for as long as your ClearTest subscription is active** and the app is installed in your Jira instance.
- If you uninstall ClearTest or your subscription ends, your data is **deleted within 90 days** of that event.
- You may request earlier deletion at any time — see [Section 7](#7-your-rights) below.

---

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

- Encrypted data in transit (TLS) and at rest
- Access controls limiting who within Steelyman Lab can access production data
- Regular security reviews

No system is 100% secure, but we are committed to handling your data responsibly and will notify affected users promptly in the event of a breach.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the following rights over your data:

**Under GDPR (EU/UK users):**
- **Access** — Request a copy of the data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — Ask us to correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — Ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restriction** — Ask us to limit how we process your data
- **Portability** — Request your data in a machine-readable format
- **Objection** — Object to our processing of your data

**Under CCPA (California users):**
- **Know** — Request details about the personal information we collect and how it is used
- **Delete** — Request deletion of your personal information
- **Non-discrimination** — We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in [Section 8](#8-contact--deletion-requests). We will respond within **30 days**.
