# Privacy Policy — PyEnv Doctor

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Marketplace:** Visual Studio Code Marketplace
**Effective Date:** June 9, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

PyEnv Doctor is a Visual Studio Code extension created by Steelyman Lab. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information (if any) is collected when you use PyEnv Doctor, how it is used, and what rights you have.

**Short version: PyEnv Doctor collects no data whatsoever.** We have no servers, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

**None.**

PyEnv Doctor does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, usage data, telemetry, or diagnostic information. All operations performed by the extension run entirely on your local machine.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- No personal identifiers (name, email, IP address, device ID)
- No usage or behavioral analytics
- No crash reports or error logs sent externally
- No environment or system information transmitted off your device
- No VS Code workspace or file content accessed beyond local execution

---

## 3. Why We Collect Data

We do not collect data, so there is no purpose to describe.

---

## 4. How Long Data Is Kept

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to retain. No retention period applies.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

PyEnv Doctor does not integrate with, call out to, or share any information with third-party services, APIs, or analytics platforms.

The only external interaction involved in using this extension is downloading it through the [Visual Studio Code Marketplace](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/), which is operated by Microsoft. Microsoft's own privacy practices govern that interaction and are described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR and CCPA

### GDPR (European Users)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. Because we collect no personal data, these rights have nothing to act upon. There is no profile, record, or data associated with your use of PyEnv Doctor on our end.

### CCPA (California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information, so no action is required on our part to comply with a request.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

PyEnv Doctor does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). No special provisions are required because no data collection occurs.
