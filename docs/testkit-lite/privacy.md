# Privacy Policy — TestKit Lite

**Publisher:** Steelyman Lab
**Effective Date:** June 13, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

TestKit Lite is a test-management app that runs inside Atlassian products (Jira). Steelyman Lab ("we", "us", or "our") built this app to help teams write, run, and track software tests. This policy explains what personal and project data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have over it.

We keep things simple: we collect only what we need to make the app work, and we don't sell your data to anyone.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

When you use TestKit Lite, we store the following information in our systems:

| Data Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Test case content** | The title, steps, expected results, and any other text you author inside a test case |
| **Test execution results** | Pass/fail status, execution date/time, and any outcome details recorded when a test is run |
| **Execution comments** | Free-text comments you add to a test execution |
| **Linked Jira issue keys** | The keys of any Jira issues (e.g., `PROJ-123`) you attach to a test case or execution |
| **User account IDs** | The Atlassian account ID of each person who runs a test or is assigned as a tester |

We do **not** collect passwords, payment details, email addresses, or any data from outside the Atlassian environment you install the app into.

---

## 3. Why We Collect This Data

Each piece of data serves a specific purpose:

- **Test case content** — to store and display the tests you create, so your team can access and reuse them.
- **Test execution results & comments** — to give your team a history of test runs, flag failures, and support quality reporting.
- **Linked Jira issue keys** — to connect test results to the bugs or stories they relate to, enabling traceability inside Jira.
- **User account IDs** — to record who executed a test, display tester names in the UI, and support accountability within your team.

We process this data on the legal basis of **contract performance** (providing the service you've installed) and, where applicable, **legitimate interests** (improving reliability and preventing abuse).

---

## 4. How Long We Keep Your Data

| Situation | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Active app installation | Data is retained for as long as the app remains installed in your Atlassian site |
| App uninstalled | All associated data is permanently deleted within **30 days** of uninstallation |
| Deletion request submitted | Data is permanently deleted within **14 days** of a verified request (see Section 8) |

We do not archive or back up your data beyond these periods for any commercial purpose.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
- Access controls limiting who on our team can access production data
- Regular dependency and security updates

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected customers within **72 hours** as required by applicable law.

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR and CCPA

Depending on where you or your users are located, the following rights may apply.

### GDPR (European Economic Area, UK, Switzerland)

- **Access** — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — Ask us to correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure ("right to be forgotten")** — Ask us to delete your personal data.
- **Restriction** — Ask us to stop actively processing your data while a dispute is resolved.
- **Portability** — Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Objection** — Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

### CCPA (California Residents)

- **Know** — Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected.
- **Delete** — Request deletion of your personal information (subject to certain exceptions).
- **Non-discrimination** — We will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.

We do **not** sell personal information as defined under the CCPA.

### Atlassian Workspace Admins

If you are a Jira site administrator, you can trigger the deletion of all data for your entire installation by uninstalling TestKit Lite and submitting a deletion request (see below).
